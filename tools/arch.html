How it works · 8 stages, one pipeline for every channel (as built)
1Entry Channels
How does a request come in?
  • WhatsApp (no app to install): text, voice note, photo of a petition, live location, in Kannada / English
  • Two-way: officer questions, decisions and resolutions arrive back in the same chat; the citizen replies, confirms 👍/👎 or appeals
  • Same pipeline for the 1902 call centre, Praja Seva camps, Grama One / CSC counters and the app
  • Excel / CSV upload for departments with spreadsheets; officer web console

2Intake & Privacy
What did the citizen say?
  • Speech-to-text (Kannada / English) on the government's own model gateway
  • Vision model reads the petition photo; unreadable or unrelated images are refused, never guessed
  • Language detection + one-line English summary
  • Masks phone, Aadhaar, email and card numbers before any AI step
  • Pulls out Sakala GSC numbers and links them to the application

3Validate & Complete
Is it complete and clean?
  • Village / GP resolved from the message (Kannada names transliterated), GPS, or a pick-list; taluk-level services skip the village question
  • Duplicate check: same service, same GP → linked, not re-filed
  • Per-service checklist for Sakala applications (name, village, document photo)
  • Wrong-door check: RTI, other departments → told where to go
↺ AI agent keeps asking the citizen until the application is complete. Sakala's legal clock starts only then.

4AI Understanding
WHAT is the problem?
  • Finds the 5 most similar real grievances (9,999 from 3–9 Oct 2026)
  • The AI model picks Department → Line Dept → Service → Grievance from the official master only (enum-constrained)
  • A second, offline model trained on the real data must agree; keyword rules override for RTI / other departments / new applications
  • Confidence below a threshold tuned from measured accuracy → scrutiny desk with the top-3 options
  • Vulnerability flag: elderly, widow, disabled, pension, ration
Solves UC 6 · 9

5Route to Officer
WHO owns it, here?
  • Service → officer role learned from the real assignments (Khata → Tahsildar 100%, water → AE RDWS 100%, street light → PDO 97%)
  • Role + taluk + GP → one named officer: PDO of that GP, Tahsildar / ADLR / Food Inspector / AEE of that taluk
  • 97.6% agreement with the real iPGRS officer on 673 Mysuru cases; 92.5% end to end from text alone
  • Every decision stores its evidence: confidence, threshold, rules, similar cases, location source, officer rule
⚑ Unsure, outside the master, or village unknown? → Scrutiny desk (L2/L3) decides and re-routes; the citizen is told. Never a silent guess.

6Deadlines & Alerts
Is the officer acting on time?
  • Grievance deadlines per level (7 / 5 / 3 days, assumed) + the Sakala legal clock for applications
  • Due-tomorrow reminders, then automatic 3-level escalation; the citizen is told each time
  • Officer inbox: open cases, days left, vulnerable first, accept / ask info / return / reject (reason mandatory) / resolve
  • ₹20/day (max ₹500) ordered by the Competent Officer → Form C notice → HRMS debit (mock)
  • Officer messages translated to Kannada for Kannada chats

7Resolution Check
Was it really solved?
  • AI compares the complaint with the officer's Action Taken Report
  • Score 5 Fully resolved … 1 Irrelevant; below 4 the case stays open and is flagged
  • AI drafts a first ATR the officer edits
  • Citizen asked on WhatsApp "was it solved?" · 👎 re-opens the case and informs the senior
↺ Not resolved, or the citizen says no? → re-opened, back to stage 6 with the senior officer informed.

D · Guardrails & what comes next
  • AI chooses only from official masters; anything else goes to a person
  • Auto-route threshold tuned from measured accuracy; below it a human decides with top-3 options
  • Every routing decision keeps its evidence (confidence, rules, similar cases, officer rule) for audit
  • AI mode answers only from data tools; instruction-override and off-data prompts are refused and logged
  • Unreadable photos / silent audio are refused, never guessed; every AI call is logged for weekly review
  • A chat sees only its own cases; only the owner acts, only the Competent Officer compensates; ladders never mix
  • Rejection needs a reason and the citizen is told the appeal officer (reply APPEAL); 👎 re-opens
  • Per-client rate limits and size caps, as in the sister POCs; all rendered text sanitised
