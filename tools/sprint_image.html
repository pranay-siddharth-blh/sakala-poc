Sovereign English Mitra  ·  The ten-day sprint, 27 Oct → 5 Nov 2026

Mitra's English voice moves from Gemini Live to our own native full-duplex core (PersonaPlex 7B, Moshi family) on the two H200s in the office server, trained by us, with the Gemini key removed from the English path on day 9. Four tracks, three gates, one buffer day. Everything else in Mitra (router, SQL, guard, read-back, pipeline, every suite) stays as it is; Kannada stays on Gemini until its own programme.

The three gates, each with a written fallback
Gate A · Thu 30 Oct 18:30 · a sovereign English turn end to end
Pass: a question on the web desk → brain → function → result spoken by the core in Mitra's voice while the mic stays open; first word < 0.6 s; the id equals the result. Signed on a recording.
If missed: same-voice fallback that evening: Kyutai TTS 1.6B on the same voice embedding streamed into the output while the core keeps listening. Full duplex kept; a small voice mismatch on facts.
Gate B · Sat 1 Nov 18:30 · suites green, FDB at PersonaPlex's level
Pass: relay 15, live-UI 10, turns 24, nav 22, bank 113, guardrails 59 green on round-1 weights; FDB v1 ≥ published PersonaPlex; 5-listener panel within 0.5 of Gemini.
If missed: the load test moves to day 7; round-2 data is cut to what the failures need.
Gate C · Tue 4 Nov 18:30 · atlas in tolerance, panel within a third of a point
Pass: every atlas behaviour inside its tolerance on round-2 weights; 20-listener blind A/B within 0.33 on naturalness, not below on correctness; FDB held.
If missed: the best weights are frozen anyway, the gap is written on the scorecard, the rehearsal goes ahead with the gap stated. A third round is a follow-on, not a slip.
The plan has no slack after day 8: day 9 is the only buffer. A missed gate is written the same evening with its fallback, never deferred.
